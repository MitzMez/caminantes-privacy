Account & Data Deletion

Caminantes · developed by AFK Ventures

You can permanently delete your Caminantes account and all of your associated data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open Caminantes and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm in the dialog. Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Don't have access to the app?

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you:

  • contact.afkventures@gmail.com

Please send the request from the same email you used to sign in (Google sign-in) so we can verify ownership. We process these requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — username, country, and profile picture
  • Your planned routes and daily stages
  • Your walk history, recorded tracks, and notes
  • Your app settings and preferences
  • Your point-of-interest contributions, reviews and bedbug reports, including any photos you uploaded
  • Your sign-in credentials (the account itself)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

What is retained

The text of messages you sent to other people is not deleted. It stays in the conversations it was sent to, because a conversation belongs to everyone in it: deleting one person’s half would rewrite the other participants’ record of an exchange they took part in. Your identity is removed from it, not merely hidden — your name, your picture and the internal account reference stored on each message are all deleted, so a kept message is not attributable to you or to any account and is shown as sent by a former caminante. You can also delete any message yourself, for everyone, within 5 minutes of sending it.

The anonymous, aggregated point-of-interest information derived from your contributions stays — an average rating, or a consensus on whether a place has a kitchen — because once combined it no longer identifies anyone. Your individual contributions, reviews and bedbug reports are deleted.

Apart from the message text described above, we do not keep your personal data after deletion. Residual copies in routine encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We retain no analytics or advertising data, because the app collects none.
