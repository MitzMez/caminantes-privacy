Account & Data Deletion

Caminantes · developed by AFK Ventures

You can permanently delete your Caminantes account and all of your associated data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open Caminantes and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm in the dialog. Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Don't have access to the app?

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you:

  • contact.afkventures@gmail.com

Please send the request from the same email you used to sign in (Google sign-in) so we can verify ownership. We process these requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — username, country, and profile picture
  • Your planned routes and daily stages
  • Your walk history, recorded tracks, and notes
  • Your app settings and preferences
  • Your sign-in credentials (the account itself)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

What is retained

Messages you sent to other people are not deleted. They stay in the conversations they were sent to, shown without your name or picture, because a conversation belongs to everyone in it: deleting one person’s half would rewrite the other participants’ record of an exchange they took part in. You can delete any message yourself, for everyone, within 5 minutes of sending it.

Community contributions you have already submitted (point-of-interest information and photos) also remain, under the licence in our Terms of Service.

Apart from those, we do not keep your personal data after deletion. Residual copies in routine encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We retain no analytics or advertising data, because the app collects none.
